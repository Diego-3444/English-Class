How to play

  1. Roll the die and move your token around the world map.
  2. The tile you land on gives you a challenge card. Everything is done in English.
  3. Speaking cards are timed. Writing cards have a minimum word count.
  4. When you finish, the other players vote: challenge passed (+10 points) or not.
  5. Landing on a city (Tokyo, Cairo, Paris, Lima, New York) triggers a big City Challenge. Pass it and you earn that city's passport stamp (+25 points).
  6. First traveller with all 5 stamps who then returns Home wins the World Tour.
  7. Missed a city? Keep going around — you can try it again next lap.
